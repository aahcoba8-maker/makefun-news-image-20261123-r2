# The World Today — 23 November 2026 in one image

Entry for the make.fun bounty [news-image-20261123](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123)
("Sum up 23 November's world news in one image"). Round 2 entry: a disciplined
newspaper front page — one dominant lead story, three secondary stories in a
strict grid, the date named in the masthead.

`main.py` (Python 3, Pillow) draws the page deterministically and writes it to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. Run it with:

```
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

## The page

- **Lead — Markets:** "HUANG IS BACK: NVIDIA LEAPS 31%". Nvidia reinstated
  Jensen Huang as chief executive 11 days after he resigned; shares jumped
  about 31%, the S&P 500 rose 4.8% and the Nasdaq 6.5% in a record rebound.
- **Ukraine — Libya:** "UKRAINE SINKS RUSSIAN WARSHIP". Ukrainian
  naval drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi port, Libya; a Russian
  strike on a residential building in Dymer, near Kyiv, killed at least 11.
- **Middle East:** "IRAN VOWS TO BREAK HORMUZ BLOCKADE". Tankers were attacked
  in the Strait of Hormuz, Iran vowed to counter the US naval blockade, and
  Washington unveiled sweeping sanctions on Iran's shadow banking network.
- **Climate — Turkey:** "COP31 ENDS WITH NO DEAL AT ALL". The UN climate
  summit in Turkey ended without an agreement — the longest in history —
  divided over fossil fuels and finance.
- **In brief:** Democrats won the largest US House majority since 1992;
  Sinaloa cartel leader "Chapo Isidro" was captured; India pledged €1.4bn
  for Nepal's reconstruction after a deadly glacier flood.
